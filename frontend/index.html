Frequently Asked Questions
What is SkillBridge?
SkillBridge is an intelligent academic & industry collaboration platform that bridges the gap between university students seeking production-grade experience and Indonesian MSMEs (UMKM) needing digital solutions like web development, mobile apps, branding, and digital marketing.
How do students apply for UMKM projects?
Students can explore the project marketplace, see required skills, evaluate AI-driven suitability scores based on their profile, and submit a 1-click tailored application with their portfolio highlights.
How does the milestone workspace work?
Once accepted, a collaboration workspace is spawned featuring an interactive 4-stage sprint roadmap, live task checklists with due dates, asset sharing, and direct messaging between student and UMKM.
How do UMKMs post a project brief?
UMKMs can post projects through a guided brief wizard by defining project objectives, deliverables, timeline, required skill tags, and offered compensation/stipend.
How does the AI Matchmaking Engine work?
Our engine leverages Google Gemini 3.8 Flash to evaluate applicant skills against project brief requirements, generating a semantic compatibility percentage, skill overlap rationale, and actionable recommendations.
How is student and UMKM data protected?
SkillBridge utilizes enterprise-grade data security with Row-Level Security (RLS) on PostgreSQL, encrypted document verification storage, and admin moderation audit trails.
